About html2frame

html2frame turns the pages of a website into editable Figma designs: real frames, text layers, vectors and images, at the screen sizes you choose, in every language and theme of the site. Everything runs on your computer; your site, its test logins and the captures never leave it.

Report a problem

First look at the usual problems on the Help page. Still stuck? Report it on GitHub: the button opens a new report with your setup already filled in. Say what you did and what went wrong, and add a screenshot if you can. You need a free GitHub account.

Don't paste passwords, and don't attach pages or captures that must stay private: reports on GitHub are public.
