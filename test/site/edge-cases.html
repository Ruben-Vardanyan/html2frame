Links and bold words in a paragraph

A paragraph with bold words, a link that wraps onto the next line, and a badge after which the text goes on and wraps over more lines.

Expected: one text layer from the start to the badge, with the bold words and the link as styled ranges, and one after the badge whose first line starts right of it (paragraph indent). No words on top of each other.

Expected: the orange box rotated −8° with its text, the blue box at 70 % size (text included). Rotation, scale and translate are kept; skew is not.

Text shadow, blend, filter

Expected: an orange text shadow (drop shadow on the text layer), the blue box in Multiply blend mode, the green box with a layer blur. Other filters (brightness, grayscale…) have no Figma equivalent.
